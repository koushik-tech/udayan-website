Who We Are

Connecting Generations, Building Careers

Founded in 1950, our social organization has been dedicated to the holistic development and cultural enrichment of the community for over seven decades. With a strong commitment to service and inclusivity, we strive to uplift people across all age groups through education, healthcare, and cultural engagement.

Our organization operates a range of departments including a charitable dispensary, a well-equipped library, and various training schools for dance, recitation, art, and karate. We also promote physical and mental well-being through yoga sessions for children and pranayama programs for senior citizens.

Beyond our regular activities, we actively engage in community welfare initiatives. Each year, we organize free health camps, distribute clothes to underprivileged children during the festive season of Durga Puja, and host cultural programs and competitions that encourage talent and bring the community together.

Driven by a spirit of service and tradition, we continue to work tirelessly to create a positive and enriching environment for all.
